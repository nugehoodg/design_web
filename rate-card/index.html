FAQ & POLICIES

How do pricing and payments work?
Projects start with a confirmed scope and deposit before production begins. A 50% upfront deposit is required to secure your slot and kick off the work. The remaining balance is settled before final file delivery.
Do you accept custom requests?
Yes. Projects outside these packages are quoted separately. Reach out with your brief and I'll put together a custom proposal tailored to your needs.
How many revisions are included?
Each package includes the revision rounds stated on its card. Additional rounds beyond the included limit can be added via the Add-ons in the calculator above.
Do you provide source files?
Source files are available as an add-on unless otherwise specified in the package. When selected, originals (PSD, AI, Figma, or .blend) are delivered alongside the final exports.
Do you provide development services?
Web development can be discussed separately. If you need frontend implementation of the web designs (HTML, CSS, JS), we can expand the scope to include development deliverables.
